## Notes

- **Temple 13 (Sensō-ji / Asakusa)** is optional. It can be done anytime from Shinjuku — easiest to slot into a free evening.
- **Car rental is only Nov 11.** Pick up in **Mito**, drive to **Temple 22** (Hitachiota) and **Temple 21** (Mt. Yahata / Daigo), then return the car in **Mito**.
- **Return to Shinjuku:** Nov 5 only, plus optionally Nov 14 if you skip the Tateyama overnight. If you stay in Tateyama, return Nov 15.
- **Hotel nights:** Nov 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, and 14 = **11 nights** if you stay in Tateyama; **10 nights** if you return late on Nov 14.
