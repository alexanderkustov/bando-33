## Daylight Notes

- **You lose about 25 minutes of daylight over the two weeks.** On Nov 2, you have roughly **10h 40m** of daylight; by Nov 15, it's down to about **10h 16m**. This is a gradual change, so it won't feel abrupt day to day.
- **The 4:00 PM goshuin deadline is the real constraint, not sunset.** Sunset is around 4:35–4:45 PM throughout the trip, so you'll have natural light for another 30–40 minutes after the stamp offices close. This means you can still enjoy the temple grounds briefly after getting your goshuin.
- **Sunrise times are early enough for a 9:00 AM start.** You'll have 2–3 hours of daylight before the temples open, which gives you a comfortable morning buffer for travel, breakfast, and any early train connections.
- **Twilight matters in rural areas.** In places like Mt. Yahata (Temple 21) or rural Chiba, it gets dark quickly after sunset. If you're driving or walking to a remote temple, plan to be back at your hotel or station before civil twilight ends (roughly 30 minutes after sunset).
