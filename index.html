At the desk (納経軸)

Nōkyō-jiku o kudasai.
I’d like a hanging scroll. (buying)
Kore wa nōkyō-chō ja nakute, nōkyō-jiku desu. Kore ni onegaishimasu.
This isn’t the book — it’s the hanging scroll. Please stamp this.
Nōkyō-jiku ni onegaishimasu.
Please stamp the hanging scroll.
Chigaimasu — jiku desu.
Wrong one — it’s the scroll.
Ikura desu ka? · Arigatō gozaimasu.
How much? · Thank you.
